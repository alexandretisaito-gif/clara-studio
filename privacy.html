Privacy Policy — CLARA STUDIO

Effective Date: November 2026

CLARA STUDIO respects user privacy and only accesses data explicitly authorized via TikTok OAuth 2.0 (such as basic profile info: open_id, display_name, avatar_url, and content publishing permissions).

1. Data Usage

OAuth tokens and basic profile identifiers are stored locally by the authorized studio operator solely to upload and publish approved media to the connected TikTok account.

2. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties. Users may revoke access at any time through their TikTok account security settings.

3. Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the operator via GitHub.
